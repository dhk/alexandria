The search results support a general conclusion that O'Farrell used a **larger, separate grid south of Market Street**, not 50-vara blocks throughout the area; however, the exact waterfront–1st Street transition could not be verified from a viewed map image.

## 2. Block sizes south of Market Street

The available descriptions support this interpretation:

- O'Farrell retained or regularized the smaller preexisting grid north of Market and laid out a **separate, wider grid south of Market**.
- One planning-history description gives the approximate dimensions as **46 yards** for the northern lots and **92 yards** for the southern lots—roughly 50-vara and 100-vara scales, respectively.
- A separate historical summary describes the south-of-Market grid as having larger blocks of approximately **600 by 400 feet**, aligned with Mission Street.

Therefore, the evidence does **not** support the proposition that the blocks between the Yerba Buena Cove waterfront and 1st Street are small 50-vara blocks matching the grid north of Market while only the area west of 1st Street becomes large. The available descriptions instead characterize the **south-of-Market grid generally as the larger grid**.

The map image was **not viewed**. This conclusion is inferred from archival and planning descriptions, not from direct inspection of the 1847 map.

## 3. Descriptions addressing the transition

- The San Francisco Planning Department describes O'Farrell as laying out "a separate grid" south of Market with lots approximately twice the width of those in the northern grid.
- SPUR describes the O'Farrell Plan as creating "a second, larger grid to the south."
- A historical account says O'Farrell's survey included the waterfront and, south of Market, showed four full blocks fronting 4th Street and eleven full blocks fronting 2nd Street, but it does **not** specify a small-to-large block transition at 1st Street.
- The OAC description says O'Farrell added Market Street diagonally and established streets in the northern area, but it does **not** describe block dimensions south of Market.

No located archival or scholarly description explicitly states that the waterfront-to-1st-Street blocks were 50-vara blocks and that the blocks west of 1st Street were 100-vara blocks. That specific claim remains **unverified**.
