# Source material

The investigation was prompted by the announcement of a Lean-only proof of the
Riemann hypothesis, produced with Claude, and by the coverage that followed. The
primary artifacts it starts from:

- The community repository: <https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis>
- Mathlib's statement of `RiemannHypothesis`, at tag v4.33.1:
  <https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/v4.33.1/Mathlib/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta.lean>
- The Lean FRO statement: <https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/>
- Andrew Sutherland's verification status page: <https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/>
- Kevin Buzzard's checklist: <https://xenaproject.wordpress.com/2026/11/12/and-the-proof-is-checked-in-lean/>
- Levent Alpöge's note: <https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf>
- The Clay Institute's problem page, which still lists RH as unsolved:
  <https://www.claymath.org/millennium/riemann-hypothesis/>

The full source list, ranked by position in the source hierarchy, is in
`03-runs/claude-corpus-2026-11-21/12_sources.md`.
