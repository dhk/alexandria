Task
Independently evaluate the proposed Agentic Organization OS. Determine whether its lifecycle, learning, governance, and organizational abstractions are technically credible and identify the narrowest architecture worth prototyping.

Context
The attached brief proposes managing autonomous LLM bots through explicit roles, onboarding, scheduled work, feedback, training, skill evolution, reassignment, and offboarding. The research will inform a 90-day prototype decision.

Constraints
Distinguish demonstrated evidence from analogy and speculation. Evaluate simpler single-agent and deterministic-workflow alternatives. Analyze security, collective alignment, feedback manipulation, skill drift, economics, and operational failure modes. Prefer primary sources. Model agreement is not factual verification.

Output needs
Produce key claims and evidence strength; architectural recommendations; unsupported assumptions; major risks; alternatives; measurable prototype hypotheses; go/no-go criteria; and prioritized open questions. Preserve material disagreement among research models.
