# Publication review

Status: publishable as Bronze technical feasibility research with prior-art gap  
Reviewed: 2026-07-30

## Checks

- Successful three-model run preserved with raw outputs and checksums.
- Brief revision and exact commission prompt preserved.
- Normalized claims and scores separated from raw evidence.
- Interpretation separated from model-generated synthesis.
- Disagreement, silence, and failed observations retain distinct semantics.
- Agreement is not presented as factual verification.
- Cost, model identities, timestamps, and generation identifiers are recorded.

## Assurance boundary

This investigation is Bronze, not Silver. The models evaluated supplied material;
they did not independently audit each cited primary source. No targeted follow-up
commission or domain-expert review has been completed. The deliverable may support
prototype scoping, but it does not validate production reliability or economics.

## Known omission

The commission did not systematically identify or compare existing solutions. It
cannot support a build-versus-buy decision. Publication must retain this limitation
until a separate market-landscape commission is completed and synthesized with this
technical assessment.
