# Topic record

## Premise

Create an agentic LLM framework that manages autonomous bots like an organization.
Bots have job descriptions, are onboarded and offboarded, perform scheduled work,
learn from human and peer feedback, pursue structured development, and receive
organization-wide training. Skills and scheduled prompts are created from the job
description and evolve through evidence from actual work.

## Intended decision

Determine whether this organizational framing is technically credible and identify
the narrowest architecture worth testing in a 90-day prototype.
