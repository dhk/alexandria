# Research questions for a Silver-level pass

These are the open questions this Bronze pass could not close — either
because `WebFetch` was blocked for the relevant domains in this session,
or because they require judgment/evidence a single search-snippet pass
can't provide. A Silver pass (multi-model, source-audited, dispatched via
`begin_research`/`run_research`) would add independent verification and a
claim landscape; none of that was run here.

1. **Direct-fetch verification of the two structured job boards' actual
   newsletter format.** Does Analytics Engineering Jobs' weekly
   newsletter carry any per-listing personal commentary, or is it purely
   tagged/structured listings as the brief and this pass both assume from
   search snippets? Same question for Data Elixir's job-board integration
   into its editorial newsletter. `analyticsengineeringjobs.com` was
   blocked by this session's egress proxy — a Silver pass with unblocked
   fetch, or a manual newsletter subscription, should confirm directly.

2. **Saturday Data Drop's actual reach and trajectory.** Subscriber
   count, current cadence, and whether it has added any editorial/
   personal-commentary layer since the archive pages found in this pass
   (April–July 2025). This is the closest surface-level (name + cadence
   + niche) collision found; worth knowing whether it's growing,
   stagnant, or already folding into something bigger.

3. **Odd Jobs (imagined/Ritvik) — current status and any analytics
   coverage.** Confirm whether it's still actively publishing, its actual
   subscriber base, and whether it has ever run analytics/data roles
   despite its general tech/startup focus. This is the closest mechanism
   match found (network-sourced, weekly, "jobs are whispered not
   published" thesis) and deserves a closer look, including whether
   there's anything to learn from how it recruits its network of
   forwarders.

4. **dbt Labs' newsletter roadmap.** Given the Fivetran merger reported
   in search results and dbt Labs' existing 50–100k-member community
   infrastructure, is there any public signal (job postings, product
   announcements, community requests) that dbt Labs is heading toward a
   curated-opportunities feature of its own? This is the single biggest
   incumbent risk to the position and deserves direct confirmation rather
   than inference.

5. **Base rates for a new curated newsletter's first 6–8 weeks.** The
   brief's "evidence of promise" signals (voluntary re-submission,
   forwards, replies, sustained readership when not job-hunting) are
   qualitatively right but uncalibrated — what should Dave actually
   expect in week 3 versus week 6 from a cold-started list of a few dozen
   people, based on comparable creator-led newsletter launches?

6. **Liability/exposure of the "what I would ask" language.** Even
   without guaranteeing outcomes, does publishing named-employer editorial
   commentary — including pointed questions a candidate "should
   investigate" — carry any meaningful defamation or reputational-harm
   exposure Dave should understand before launch? Scope: informational
   only, not legal advice: what would a careful non-lawyer editorial
   practice look like here (e.g. sourcing/attribution discipline,
   correction policy) and when would this actually need a lawyer.

7. **Network concentration in the seed group.** Of the 15–20 people the
   pilot plans to ask directly, how concentrated are they by employer,
   function, and era of Dave's career? A skewed seed group would bias
   early issue coverage in a way the target audience — experienced,
   discerning analytics practitioners — would likely notice. Worth an
   explicit pre-launch check, not just organic pilot feedback.

## What this pass deliberately did not re-litigate

The brief's editorial lens (the seven-question framework), annotation
vocabulary, product shape, explicit exclusions, and six-week pilot design
are execution decisions, not competitive-landscape claims — this Bronze
pass only stress-tested the market-positioning argument. They're sound as
written and out of scope for a Silver research pass; any future revision
to them belongs in a new brief version per this repo's `AGENTS.md`
("Research changes" section), not in this investigation.
