# Research brief: Agentic Organization OS existing solutions and gap analysis

Status: draft for commission review  
Date: 2026-08-06  
Perspective: `market-landscape@1.0.0`  
Required source mode: `web-discovery`

## Task

Identify commercial products, open-source frameworks, standards, and composable
infrastructure that already solve the Agentic Organization OS premise in whole or in
part. Determine what, if anything, remains genuinely unsolved and recommend whether
to build, buy, integrate, partner, defer, or stop.

## Context

The premise is a governed agentic system in which bots have machine-readable job
descriptions, are onboarded and offboarded, receive skills and scheduled work from
their roles, learn through human and peer feedback, undergo controlled training, and
evolve their prompts and skills through evidence-gated change.

A prior technical assessment found the component mechanisms credible but the
integrated organization unproven. It did not perform a current solution landscape.
This commission exists specifically to correct that omission.

## Capability model

Evaluate each solution against:

1. Role and job-description contracts
2. Bot identity and lifecycle state
3. Onboarding and qualification
4. Skill generation, storage, retrieval, and versioning
5. Scheduled and event-driven work
6. Delegation and multi-agent coordination
7. Human, peer, environmental, and self-feedback
8. Evaluation, tracing, and performance scorecards
9. Evidence-gated prompt and skill evolution
10. Organizational training and shared playbooks
11. Permissions, budgets, credentials, and approval gates
12. Suspension, revocation, reassignment, and offboarding
13. Memory scope, retention, and deletion
14. Organization-level governance and collective-behavior controls

## Search scope

Cover complete platforms and reasonable combinations across these categories:

- Agent orchestration and multi-agent frameworks
- Managed agent runtimes and workforce platforms
- Workflow schedulers and durable execution systems
- Agent skills, memory, and learning systems
- Evaluation, observability, governance, and guardrail products
- Identity, secrets, permission, and policy infrastructure for agents
- Standards and interoperability protocols relevant to portable agents and skills

Include both commercial and open-source candidates. Search beyond candidates named
in the supplied material. Record excluded and unresolved candidates so absence is
not mistaken for nonexistence.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer official documentation, repositories, release notes, standards, and papers.
- Cite every material capability claim with a direct link.
- Distinguish generally available, preview, announced, deprecated, and inferred.
- Do not treat marketing language as proof of implementation.
- Assess evidence freshness as of 2026-08-06.
- Agreement among research models is not factual verification.

## Output

1. Solution taxonomy and candidate inventory
2. Capability matrix using only `solved`, `partially_solved`, `adjacent`,
   `not_found`, or `unknown`
3. Evidence and maturity notes for every material verdict
4. Complete-solution and composable-stack options
5. Genuinely unresolved gaps
6. Build, buy, integrate, partner, defer, or stop recommendation
7. Search limitations and candidates requiring follow-up
8. Validation plan for the recommended path

A build recommendation is prohibited while a material prior-art gap remains.
