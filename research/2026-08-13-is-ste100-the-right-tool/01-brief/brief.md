Context
This is an iteration on run r-2026-0813-01, published as 2026-08-13-is-ste100-the-right-tool. That run concluded — unanimously across three models — that ASD-STE100 is not a general-purpose writing standard and is actively harmful for persuasion, because it removes by design the devices persuasion depends on. It also concluded that STE's current popularity in AI and writing-tool circles derives from its machine-operability rather than from anything connected to its origin. Two weaknesses in it are the reason for this brief: the provenance question was answered from model recall and the models disagreed with each other, and the comparison set was drawn from the literature rather than from the tools people actually use. Where this run contradicts the prior one, say so directly and explain which is better supported — a correction is more useful than consistency.
